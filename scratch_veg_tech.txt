1. ANALYSIS OVERVIEW & STATUS

This technical document audits the single-date multispectral vegetation inference pipeline. The pipeline processes four calibrated spectral
bands (Green, Red, RedEdge, NIR) through a deep-learning segmentation engine (ResNet34 U-Net) to generate pixel-level categorical
canopy condition masks and continuous confidence surfaces. Temporal change, transitions, and hydrological indices are excluded in
single-date mode.

8. SCIENTIFIC METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONS
Methodological Formulation: Single-date vegetation assessment uses normalized spectral band ratios sensitive to the red-edge inflection point (~700–740 nm), which
responds directly to foliar nitrogen and chlorophyll-a/b absorption. The deep learning segmentation architecture resolves non-linear spectral mixing inherent in mixed ground
pixels (10 m / 20 m Ground Sample Distance).
Assumptions & Constraints:
 Non-temporal: Single-acquisition analysis cannot infer long-term trends or separate seasonal senescence from permanent vegetative degradation.
 Hydrological Exclusion: Does not evaluate surface water dynamics, soil moisture saturation, or wetland boundaries.
 Radiometric Noise: Cloud edge effects and steep terrain shadow may introduce localized classification noise.
