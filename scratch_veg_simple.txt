7. METHOD SUMMARY
Vegetation health evaluation relies on standard physical spectral indices coupled with deep-learning semantic segmentation:
 NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index): Quantifies green canopy biomass and density.
 GNDVI (Green NDVI): Highly sensitive to chlorophyll concentration and foliar nutrient status.
 NDRE (RedEdge NDVI): Detects internal cellular stress before visible surface chlorosis.
 Machine Learning Model: A calibrated convolutional neural network combines these multispectral channels into categorical health classes
without subjective manual thresholds.
8. REPORT NOTES & LIMITATIONS
Single-Date Snapshot: This report reflects vegetation condition exclusively at the date and time of satellite acquisition. It does not measure change over time and cannot
establish whether stress is seasonal, drought-induced, or permanent.
No Wetland Assessment: This mode performs vegetation health classification only. It does NOT evaluate wetland boundaries, hydrological depletion, or physical wetland
loss. For multi-temporal wetland degradation analysis, run the engine in Wetland Mode with two observation dates.
Atmospheric & Ground Factors: While cloud-screened and atmospheric corrections are applied, sun angle, soil moisture, and phenology can influence reflectance values.
Ground validation is recommended for targeted site-level interventions.
